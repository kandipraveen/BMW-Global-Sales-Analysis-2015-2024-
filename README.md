# BMW Global Sales Analysis (2015–2024)
## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive exploratory data analysis (EDA) of BMW’s global sales performance from 2015 to 2024. The objective is to evaluate sales trends, identify high-performing markets and models, assess key business drivers, and generate actionable strategic insights.

The dataset consists of 1,000 structured records across 14 business variables, including pricing, marketing spend, dealership count, macroeconomic indicators, competition intensity, and units sold.

The analysis integrates statistical evaluation, visualization, and business interpretation to support strategic decision-making.

## Business Objectives

Analyze year-over-year sales growth trends

Identify top-performing regions and revenue drivers

Evaluate model-wise and segment-wise performance

Assess the impact of pricing and marketing on sales

Examine the electric vehicle growth trajectory

Understand competitive positioning

Identify seasonal sales patterns

## Key Insights
### 1. Sales Performance

Total Units Sold: 633,192

Highest Sales Year: 2015

Strong rebound observed in 2024

Sales show cyclical volatility but strong recovery capability

### 2. Regional Performance

China is the highest revenue-generating market ($6.07B)

China contributes 12.24% of total global sales

USA shows comparatively lower sales performance

### 3. Model & Segment Analysis

X3 is the best-selling and highest-revenue model

Electric segment leads total units sold

Strong growth observed in electric vehicle adoption

### 4. Pricing & Demand Dynamics

Weak negative correlation (-0.074) between price and units sold

Indicates relatively low price sensitivity

Strong positive correlation (0.74) between units sold and revenue

### 5. Marketing & Competition

Marketing spend shows minimal direct linear correlation with sales

High competition does not necessarily reduce performance

Brand strength supports competitive resilience

### 6. Seasonal Trends

Q1 and Q4 consistently outperform Q2 and Q3

February recorded highest monthly performance
